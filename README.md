📋 Features 📋
-
- A robot that can play against you 
- Point system for both you and your opponent
- Scores save before you close the game
- Points reset when someone reaches 15 points
- A robot who's speed will increase or decrease depending on how well you do
- The player's control reverse when they reach 10 points to make the game a bit more challenging


🕹️ How To Play 🕹️
-
1. Use the up and down arrow keys to move your paddle
2. The goal of the game is to hit the ball back and fourth against your opponent and score as many points as possible
3. When you reach 10 points, your controls will reverse to increase the difficulty
4. First to 15 points wins
